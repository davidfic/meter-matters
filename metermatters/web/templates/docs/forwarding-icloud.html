iCloud Mail, step by step

  • icloud.com, in a browser on a computer
  • Meter Matters, the Add bills page
  • About five minutes

iCloud Mail forwards with a rule, and needs no confirmation of the address. One rule, then a test.

Make the rule at icloud.com, not in the Mail app on a Mac. A rule made at icloud.com runs on Apple's servers, so it works whichever device you read mail on. A rule made in the Mac's Mail app runs only while that Mac is awake with Mail open.

Make the rule

  1. On {{ app("web.connect", "Add bills") }}, press Copy next to your bills address.
  2. Sign in at icloud.com and open Mail.
  3. Press the gear at the top of the folder list, then Settings, then Rules.
{{ shot("icloud-rules.png", "iCloud Mail's settings on the Rules tab, with the Add a Rule button", "The Rules tab. Add a Rule is at the top.") }}
  1. Press Add a Rule.
  2. If a message: choose is from, and paste your utility's sender address. For Eversource it is noreply@notifications.eversource.com.
  3. Then: choose Forward to, and paste your bills address.
{{ shot("icloud-rule.png", "The new rule: is from the utility's sender, forward to the bills address", "The whole rule. One condition, one action.") }}
  1. Press Add, then Done.

If gas and electric come from different senders, make a second rule the same way.

Send a test

A rule applies only to mail that arrives from now on. To see it work today, open a recent bill email, press Forward, put your bills address in To, and send. From the Mail app on an iPhone is fine. Within a minute or two, a Filed line appears under What has arrived on Add bills.

{{ shot("forwarding-filed.png", "The What has arrived list with a bill filed, marked as forwarded by you", "The test bill, filed. The next real bill will arrive through the rule on its own.") }}

Send the test from the address you signed up with. A forward from any other address is not filed.

If something is off

What you seeWhat to do
The rule exists, but no bill ever arrives Rules apply to new mail only; send a test by hand. Check the rule is at icloud.com, not only in the Mac's Mail app.
The test says Forwarded by …, who isn't you Forward from the address you signed up with. An iCloud account can have several addresses; use the one on your Meter Matters account.
The log says Not from the utility's address for a bill the rule sent The rule's sender must be the address on the bill email exactly. Utilities use different addresses for bills, payments and offers.
I pressed Issue a new address The old address stopped working. Change the rule's Forward to address to the new one.

Anything else: the {{ doc("troubleshooting", "troubleshooting page", "email") }}.
