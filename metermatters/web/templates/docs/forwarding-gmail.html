Gmail, step by step

  • Gmail, in a browser on a computer
  • Meter Matters, the Add bills page
  • About ten minutes

Gmail will not forward to an address it has not confirmed. So there are three parts: add the address, confirm it, make the filter. Then a test.

The part people get stuck on. When you add the address, Gmail says it has sent a confirmation email. It has, to your bills address. It is not in your inbox. Meter Matters receives it and shows you Google's link on the Add bills page. Part 2 below.

Keep two tabs open: Gmail in one, {{ app("web.connect", "Add bills") }} in the other.

Part 1. Add the forwarding address in Gmail

  1. On Add bills, press Copy next to your bills address.
{{ shot("forwarding-address.png", "The address row on the card, with the Copy button", "The address is on the first card of Add bills.") }}
  1. In Gmail, press the gear at the top right, then See all settings.
{{ shot("gmail-settings-gear.png", "Gmail's Quick settings panel with the See all settings button at the top", "The gear opens Quick settings. See all settings is at the top.") }}
  1. Open the Forwarding and POP/IMAP tab.
{{ shot("gmail-forwarding-tab.png", "The Forwarding and POP/IMAP tab, with the Add a forwarding address button", "The tab. Disable forwarding stays selected: the filter will forward only the bills.") }}
  1. Press Add a forwarding address. Paste your bills address. Press Next.
{{ shot("gmail-add-address.png", "The Add a forwarding address dialog with a bills address pasted in", "Paste the whole address, from bills- to the end.") }}
  1. A window asks you to confirm. Press Proceed. Then OK.
{{ shot("gmail-confirmation-sent.png", "Gmail's notice that a confirmation code has been sent to the address", "Gmail has sent the confirmation to your bills address. Do not look for it in your inbox.") }}

Leave Disable forwarding selected on that tab. You are not forwarding your whole mailbox. The filter in Part 3 forwards only the bills.

Part 2. Confirm it, from Meter Matters

  1. Go to the Add bills tab and reload the page. The card now says Gmail's confirmation has arrived.
{{ shot("forwarding-confirm-box.png", "The box on the card with Gmail's confirmation waiting: a link to Google's page, the code, and a Done button", "The only link ever shown here is one to Google's own site.") }}
  1. Press Open Gmail's confirmation link. Google's page opens. Press Confirm.
{{ shot("google-confirm-page.png", "Google's Confirm forwarding page with a Confirm button", "Google's page. Confirm is the only thing to press.") }}
  1. Back on Add bills, press Done. Google does not tell the app when you have confirmed, so this is how it knows.
{{ shot("forwarding-confirmed.png", "The What has arrived list with one line: Confirmed, forwarding confirmed in Gmail", "After Done. The address is now one Gmail will forward to.") }}

If you would rather not follow the link, the card also shows the code. In Gmail, on the Forwarding tab, there is a box for it next to the new address. Type the code and press Verify.

Nothing on the card after a reload? Google usually sends within seconds, but mail can take a few minutes. Reload again. If there is still nothing after ten minutes, go back to Gmail's Forwarding tab, remove the address, and add it again.

Part 3. Make the filter

  1. In Gmail's search box at the top, press the sliders icon at the right. The search options open.
{{ shot("gmail-search-options.png", "Gmail's search box with the sliders icon at the right", "The sliders icon. Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter opens the same panel.") }}
  1. In From, paste your utility's sender address. For Eversource it is noreply@notifications.eversource.com. Leave every other field empty. Press Create filter, not Search.
{{ shot("gmail-filter-from.png", "Gmail's search options panel with From set to noreply@notifications.eversource.com and the Create filter button", "Only From is filled in. This is a real Gmail screenshot; the sender shown is Eversource's.") }}
  1. Tick Forward it to. Choose your bills address from the list beside it.
{{ shot("gmail-filter-actions.png", "Gmail's filter actions panel with Forward it to and an address dropdown", "Forward it to is the only box to tick.") }} {{ shot("gmail-forward-to.png", "The Forward it to box ticked, with a bills address chosen in the dropdown", "The address chosen. If it is not in the list, it has not been confirmed yet: Part 2.") }}
  1. Leave the other boxes unticked. Press Create filter.
  2. Gmail lists the filter under Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses.
{{ shot("gmail-filter-list.png", "The Filters and Blocked Addresses tab listing the new filter: from the utility, forward to the bills address", "The finished filter. Gmail adds a bar at the top of your inbox saying mail is being forwarded; that bar is about this filter.") }}

If gas and electric come from different senders, make a second filter the same way.

Part 4. Send a test

The filter applies only to mail that arrives from now on. To see it work today, open a recent bill email, press Forward, put your bills address in To, and send. Within a minute or two, a Filed line appears under What has arrived on Add bills.

{{ shot("forwarding-filed.png", "The What has arrived list with a bill filed, marked as forwarded by you", "The test bill, filed. The next real bill will arrive through the filter on its own.") }}

Send the test from the Gmail address you signed up with. A forward from any other address is not filed.

If something is off

What you seeWhat to do
Gmail says it sent a confirmation email, but it is not in my inbox It went to your bills address. Reload Add bills; the link is on the card.
My bills address is not in the Forward it to list It has not been confirmed yet. Do Part 2. The Add forwarding address link beside the list does the same as Part 1.
Gmail will not let me add a forwarding address A work or school Google account can be set by its administrator to block forwarding outside the organisation. Ask them, or forward bills by hand from any mailbox.
The filter exists but nothing has arrived Filters apply to new mail only. Wait for the next bill, or send a test by hand.
The test says Forwarded by …, who isn't you Forward from the address you signed up with.
I pressed Issue a new address The old address stopped working. Add and confirm the new one (Parts 1 and 2), then change the filter to it.

Anything else: the {{ doc("troubleshooting", "troubleshooting page", "email") }}.
