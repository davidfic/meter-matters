Forward your bill emails

Your account has an email address that is yours alone. Set your mailbox to forward your utility's "your bill is ready" emails to it. From then on, each new bill files itself.

How it works

Your utilityEmails you when a bill is ready, as it does now.
Your mailboxA filter you make sends a copy of that one sender's mail on. Nothing else leaves.
Your bills addressbills-…@…, made for you. Nobody else has it.
Meter MattersChecks the sender, reads the amount and the dates, files the bill.

The app never signs in to your mail and holds no password for it. It receives only the copies your filter sends, and it does not keep them. The {{ doc("security", "security page", "forwarding") }} goes through that in detail.

Before you start

  • A computer. Mail filters are made in a browser, not in the phone app. Ten minutes, once.
  • A recent bill email from your utility, open, so you can see who sent it.
  • Your bills address, on the first card of {{ app("web.connect", "Add bills") }}. If the card says Not available on this server, the person running the server has not switched inbound email on. Upload and by-hand still work. The {{ doc("operators", "operators page") }} says what switching it on involves.

The three steps

  1. Copy your bills address from Add bills.
  2. Tell your mailbox it may forward to that address. Gmail asks you to confirm the address first. Google's confirmation goes to the bills address, not your inbox, and Meter Matters shows it to you. Outlook and iCloud have no confirmation step.
  3. Make a filter: mail from your utility's sender, forward it to your bills address.

Then forward one bill by hand to check it all works. Each page below walks through every screen.

Pick your mail

GmailAdd the address, confirm it, make the filter. OutlookOutlook.com and Microsoft 365. One rule. iCloud MailOne rule at icloud.com.

Yahoo, or a mail app with no forwarding rule? Skip the filter and forward each bill by hand. It is one tap a month.

Your bills address

On {{ app("web.connect", "Add bills") }}, the first card is Your private bills address. The address is the word bills, a dash, and twenty-four random letters and digits. Press Copy. You will paste it into your mail in the next step.

Who sends your bills

The filter matches on the sender's address, so that only bill emails are forwarded. Open a recent "your bill is ready" email. The address is next to the sender's name at the top. On a phone, tap the name to see it.

Check that it works

A filter applies only to mail that arrives after it was made, so the first real test would be next month. Do not wait. Open a recent bill email, press Forward, put your bills address in To, and send. Within a minute or two a line appears under What has arrived on Add bills.

{{ shot("forwarding-filed.png", "The What has arrived list with one bill filed, marked as forwarded by you", "A bill forwarded by hand. The amount and dates are read the same way as from a filter.") }}

One rule: forward from the address you signed up with. A forward carries your signature, not the utility's, so your address is the only proof the app has that it was you. A forward from anywhere else is logged as Not filed, and nobody who learns your bills address can plant a bill in it.

If your bills land in a different mailbox from the one you signed up with, a filter in that mailbox still works. A filter passes the utility's own message through, signature and all.

Forward one by hand, every month

Without a filter, forwarding each bill yourself works just as well. Open the bill email, press Forward, send it to your bills address. It works from any mail app, including on a phone. The same rule applies: send it from the address you signed up with.

Questions

  • Will it see my other mail? No. A filter on one sender forwards that sender's mail. Nothing else leaves your mailbox, and the app has no way in.
  • What about bills from before today? A filter applies to new mail only. Forward old ones by hand, or {{ doc("upload", "upload") }} your utility's export, which brings in years at once.
  • Electric and gas from different senders? One filter per sender.
  • Can I do this on my phone? Making the filter needs a browser on a computer, once. Forwarding by hand works anywhere.
  • I pasted the address somewhere I should not have. Press Issue a new address at the foot of the card. The old one stops working at once. Then put the new one in your filter. In Gmail that means adding and confirming it again.
  • How do I stop? Delete the filter. In Gmail, also remove the address on the Forwarding tab. Nothing else was connected, so there is nothing else to undo.
