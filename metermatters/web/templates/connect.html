How to find the file on your utility's site

Menus move, so these say what to look for rather than exactly where it is. Once signed in, look for Green Button, Download my data, Usage history or Billing history. A CSV or XML is what you want{% if scans %}; a PDF of one bill goes through the scan route, one bill at a time{% else %}; a PDF of a bill isn't readable here yet{% endif %}.

Eversource (MA, CT, NH)
eversource.com → Billing & payments for the payment history (AccountHistory.csv); Energy usage → Download usage data for the Green Button zip. Both drop straight in here.
National Grid
nationalgridus.com → Energy usage → Download usage (Green Button). If the billing page only offers PDFs, add the bills by hand below.
PG&E
pge.com → Energy usage details → Green Button → Download my data. Pick a date range and CSV or XML. Export electric and gas separately; a file holding both is turned away with a note saying so.
Con Edison
coned.com → My Energy Use → Download My Data (Green Button). Pick a year or more.
Duke Energy
duke-energy.com → Usage → look for Download or Green Button. Budget-billed accounts still work: usage pairs to each bill by its dates.
Xcel Energy, SCE, SDG&E, Dominion
Each has Green Button or Download usage under its usage or energy pages. Any CSV with dates and amounts will do.
Water and sewer
Many water utilities offer a usage or billing export too; gallons or CCF both work. Otherwise a quarterly bill typed in by hand is enough.
Any other utility
Look for an export of your usage or bills. If we can't tell which column is which, we'll show you the file and ask. Nothing to export? Add bills by hand below - a date and an amount is all it takes.
